## Backdown

After a successful Power Attack against a melee target, they must make a Will Save DC 10 + 1/2 of your character level + Strength modifier + Intimidate  value. If they fail the saving throw, they are flat-footed for 1 round. Back down is a mind-affecting fear effect.

`3fdf198e916c46fc998f49028543e6fc`  
`Backdown`  

## Battlefield Intuition

`6aa8b689c17d488a9f6fa0a188fb15cf`  
`BattlefieldIntuition`  

## Charging Hurler

You can use the charge rules to make a thrown weapon attack. All the parameters of a charge apply, except that you must only move closer to your opponent, and you must end your movement within 30 feet of that opponent. If you do, you can make a single thrown weapon attack against that opponent, gaining the +2 bonus on the attack roll and taking a –2 penalty to your AC until the start of your next turn.

`2b43e550fcf54883856732c3eb9a2d85`  
`ChargingHurler-feat`  

## Disrupting Spells

Your magic takes on a quality which distrupts other's magic. Upon hitting a target with a spell, dispel 1 buff or debuff from the target, based on your spell DC. Note this will affect both allies and enemies.
