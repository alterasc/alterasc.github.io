## Ascendant Critical

Your mastery of critical strikes is unmatched.  
Your critical threat range and critical damage multiplier for all weapons, including unarmed strikes and natural weapons, are both increased by 1, stacking with all other similar effects.

## Hellball Spell

Grants you a new powerful spell called Hellball, which deals 20d6 each of fire, acid, electrical, and sonic damage. Hellball is a 9th-level spell.

`e7adab84d36644c6b6c30e3b33ce7af0`  
`HellBall-feature`  

## Mythic Disrupting Spells

The disrupting quality of your magic is increased by your mythic power. Upon hitting a target with a spell, dispel 5 buffs or debuffs from the target, based on your spell DC. Note this will affect both allies and enemies.

`09bb3ee416d2463b8620b36130283839`  
`mythicdisruptingspells`  

## Potent Spellcasting (Mythic)

Mythic power makes your spells almost impossible to resist.  
Add +6 to the Difficulty Class for all saving throws against spells you cast. This bonus stacks with the bonuses from Potent Spellcasting, Improved Potent Spellcasting, and Greater Potent Spellcasting.

`e20286b6e62f4e02bbe65735b8bae69e`  
`MythicSupportNethys`
